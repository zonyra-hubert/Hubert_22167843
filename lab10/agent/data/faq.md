# Course Registration FAQ (fictional, for DCIT 403 Lab 10)

## Add/drop period
Students may add or drop courses during the add/drop period, which runs for the first two weeks of the semester. After add/drop closes, courses can only be dropped with the approval of the Head of Department and the Dean, and a late-change fee applies.

## Late registration
Students who do not register by the registration deadline must pay a late registration fee of GHS 200 (fictional amount). Late registration closes at the end of the add/drop period. Students who are not registered by then cannot sit examinations.

## Credit limits
A full-time student must register for at least 15 credits and at most 24 credits per semester. To register for more than 24 credits, a student needs written approval from the Head of Department. Students on academic probation may register for at most 18 credits.

## Prerequisites
DCIT 403 (Designing Intelligent Agents) requires DCIT 201 (Programming I) and DCIT 202 (Programming II). DCIT 418 requires DCIT 403. If the portal blocks a course because of a missing prerequisite, the student must see the Level Coordinator with their transcript.

## Fees and registration
Students must pay at least 60% of their fees before they can register on the student portal. Payment can be made at partner banks or by mobile money. It can take up to 48 hours for a payment to show on the portal.

## Registration problems
If the portal shows an error, take a screenshot and report it to the Departmental Registration Desk (Room CS10, fictional) or email the registration desk. Do not pay a second time if a payment does not show; wait 48 hours and then bring the receipt.

## Courses from other departments
Students may take up to two elective courses from other departments each semester, if the timetable allows. Approval from the other department's Level Coordinator is required.

## Withdrawal and deferment
A student who wants to defer the semester must apply to the Academic Affairs office before the end of Week 6. Fees already paid are carried forward to the next semester.
