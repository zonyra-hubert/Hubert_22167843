# Asempa Community Clinic: NHIS and Payment

All information in this document is fictional and for teaching only.

## NHIS
The clinic is accredited by the National Health Insurance Scheme (NHIS). Patients with an active NHIS membership pay nothing for consultation and for medicines and laboratory tests on the NHIS list.

To use NHIS, bring your NHIS card or Ghana Card, which must be linked to an active membership. Members whose membership has expired are treated as cash patients until they renew. Renewal can be done at any NHIS district office or with the MyNHIS mobile app.

## Items not covered by NHIS
Medicines that are not on the NHIS medicines list, the lipid profile test, and cosmetic products. The pharmacy will tell you the price before dispensing.

## Cash patients
Consultation fee: GHS 50 for adults, GHS 30 for children under 12 (fictional prices). Laboratory prices are displayed at the laboratory reception.

## Payment methods
Cash and mobile money (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash and AT Money). Card payments are not accepted.
