# Asempa Community Clinic: Opening Hours

All information in this document is fictional and for teaching only.

## Outpatient department (OPD)
- Monday to Friday: 7:30am to 6:00pm
- Saturday: 8:00am to 2:00pm
- Sunday and public holidays: closed. For emergencies, go to the nearest hospital emergency unit.

## Laboratory
- Monday to Friday: 7:00am to 5:00pm. Samples for fasting tests are taken only between 7:00am and 10:00am.
- Saturday: 8:00am to 12:00 noon.
- Sunday: closed.

## Pharmacy
- Monday to Friday: 7:30am to 7:00pm
- Saturday: 8:00am to 4:00pm
- Sunday: 10:00am to 2:00pm (prescriptions from this clinic only)

## Antenatal clinic
- Tuesdays and Thursdays, 8:00am to 1:00pm. Please arrive before 10:00am.

## Child welfare clinic (weighing and immunisation)
- Wednesdays, 8:00am to 12:00 noon.

The clinic closes 30 minutes early on the last Friday of every month for staff training.
