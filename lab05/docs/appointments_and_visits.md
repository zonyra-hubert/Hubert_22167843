# Asempa Community Clinic: Appointments, Visits and Emergencies

All information in this document is fictional and for teaching only.

## Do I need an appointment?
No appointment is needed for the OPD, the laboratory or the pharmacy. Antenatal patients are given a return date at each visit. Specialist visiting doctors (eye clinic, first Monday of each month) require booking at the reception.

## Visiting a patient under observation
Patients under observation in the treatment room may have one visitor at a time, between 12:00 noon and 1:00pm and between 4:00pm and 5:00pm.

## Emergencies
The clinic is not an emergency centre. Anyone with chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, a seizure, loss of consciousness or a serious injury should go straight to the nearest hospital emergency unit or call the National Ambulance Service on 112.

## Complaints and feedback
Speak to the nurse in charge, or write to the clinic manager. Complaints are acknowledged within three working days.
