# Asempa Community Clinic: Preparing for Laboratory Tests

All information in this document is fictional and for teaching only.

## Fasting blood sugar and lipid profile
Do not eat or drink anything except water for 8 to 12 hours before the test. Come to the laboratory between 7:00am and 10:00am. Take your usual medicines unless your doctor told you otherwise, and bring them with you.

## Urine routine examination
Collect a mid-stream sample of the first urine of the morning in the sterile container given by the laboratory. Bring it to the laboratory within one hour.

## Malaria test
No preparation is needed. Tell the laboratory staff if you have taken any antimalarial medicine in the last two weeks.

## Pregnancy test
A urine sample from the first urine of the morning gives the most reliable result.

## General advice
Bring your laboratory request form from the doctor. Children should come with a parent or guardian. Results are given only to the patient, or to a parent or guardian for children.
