# Asempa Community Clinic: Services

All information in this document is fictional and for teaching only.

## General consultation
Doctors and physician assistants see adults and children at the OPD. Patients are seen in order of arrival, except for urgent cases, which the triage nurse sees first.

## Laboratory tests
Full blood count, malaria test (RDT and microscopy), fasting blood sugar, lipid profile, urine routine examination, pregnancy test, hepatitis B screening, HIV testing with counselling, and typhoid test. Results for most tests are ready the same day; lipid profile results are ready the next working day.

## Maternal and child health
Antenatal care, postnatal care, family planning counselling, child welfare clinic, immunisation according to the national schedule.

## Pharmacy
The pharmacy dispenses prescriptions from this clinic and sells common over-the-counter medicines.

## Services we do not provide
Surgery, X-ray, ultrasound scans and admission (overnight stay). Patients who need these services are referred to the district hospital with a referral letter.

## Contact
Reception: 030 000 0000 (fictional number). WhatsApp messages are answered Monday to Friday during OPD hours.
